02 The Opportunity

A new medium is exploding,
and quality is scarce

Vertical micro dramas have become one of the fastest-growing content formats in the world, while AI video has collapsed the cost of production. The result: platforms need enormous volumes of episodic content — but almost nobody can deliver it at true cinematic quality.

/ 01

Vertical drama boom

Micro-drama platforms are commissioning thousands of episodic series a year for global audiences — demand for content far outpaces supply.

/ 02

AI collapses cost

Generative video now produces broadcast-grade imagery at a fraction of traditional budgets, opening premium episodic content to new economics.

/ 03

The quality gap

Most AI content looks like AI content. Character drift, flat lighting, no visual grammar. Audiences notice — and platforms are paying for better.

06 How We Work

A production pipeline,
not a prompt box

01

Develop

Lookbooks, character turnarounds and visual bibles — every series starts with real pre-production.

02

Design

Locked character and environment assets generated to a defined photographic style, before a single shot is animated.

03

Animate

Shot-by-shot animation with timecoded direction — blocking, camera moves and performance treated like a real set.

04

Finish

Upscale, grade and conform to delivery spec. Cinematic consistency held across every episode of a season.

Consistency systems — locked source assets, character sheets and style bibles — keep faces, wardrobe and worlds identical across hundreds of shots.
